Abhay on 2026-11-06: "What you need is heavy verificaitons and rulesets, and getting intention and references as you build gradually step by step rather than forcing anything, spend a ton of time on UI developers with good mcps, figma clones, etc, as you slowly buil dcontent out." Also: "we don't need anything basic, whats much more important is the small things. Contrast, alpha channel values", and cheap models such as DeepSeek Flash can generate a whole app under heavy instruction; "The real question is just how you can guide it there".

**Why:** one-shot builds forced a result and came out thin. Vespin's look is many small values combined. Volume is cheap; guidance is the scarce part.

**How to apply:** don't one-shot screens. Work one component at a time: intention, reference, values, build alone, verify, Abhay looks, lock. Spend most of the effort on UI before filling in content. Use [[feedback-ui-taste]] and [[feedback-true-apps-scale]]. Paid runs still need a cap, see [[feedback-deepseek-spend-cap]].
