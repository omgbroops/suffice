For the site-factory redesign work, "dated" sites are the ideal aesthetic. The user said El Rancho's original was ~10x better than my rebuilt version (flat colour bands, heavy display font, generic hero), and Uncle Chin's original is "a 70".

**Why:** my modernized layouts kept reading as slop (v1-v3 Uncle Chin's, El Rancho v1); the human, pre-template look is what the user values.

**How to apply:** keep Abhay's layout, fonts, photos and wording; patch only real defects (menu in PDF, wrong hours, popups, no mobile layout, no order path). Do not use Haiku's "dated" grade as a pitch signal. See [[tandem-startup]] for the user's general no-slop taste and ~/site-factory/PROCESS.md for the written process.

**Update 2026-10-11 (Rasoi):** user rated Rasoi's original 8/10 and my minimalist concept 4/10 ("nonminimalist, non-AI-slop, genuinely nice"). Minimalism (cream bg + serif + plain text) is itself an AI tell. I over-subtract. My redesigns have never beaten originals; only Midway-style patches that keep the full look have worked. Do not build from-scratch redesigns; patch defects only, and let the user's own rating of originals drive site selection (not the Haiku "dated" grader).

**Update (Rasoi, 2026-10-11):** user rated Rasoi's original 8/10 and my minimalist rebuild 4/10: "you're subtracting the slop out of AI slop and getting robotic, soulless trash... nobody actually uses modernist." Rule: never rebuild or minimise. Mirror their page, keep photos/colour/density/fonts, patch defects, add character in their own style.
