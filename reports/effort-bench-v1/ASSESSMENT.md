# Retain High provisionally; Medium needs broader accuracy evidence

The authorized six-attempt followup is complete. Full v2 Medium passed 2/3 tasks at $0.00927542 and 63.473 seconds mean actor latency. Original v1 Luna High also passed 2/3, at $0.01598388 and 93.202 seconds. Historical full v2 High passed 3/3 at $0.02084424 and 87.049 seconds.

Medium is a promising cost/speed option, but its missing Commander compatibility repair prevents a quality-first recommendation to lower effort by default. All public checks passed, so no advice, controller repair or takeover activated; the pipeline did not detect these semantic failures itself. Keep High as a provisional candidate, not a proven winner.

The same-model comparison also prevents overstating the harness effect: v2 High cost more in total than original v1 Luna High while accepting one more repair. The configurations ran at different times on three exposed tasks with unreset cache state. Individual harness features and effort effects remain uncertain.

Receipt timing places about 96–98% of actor wall time between provider-request and assistant receipts, with deterministic checks around 0.25 seconds. This includes network/queueing/inference/streaming, not measured provider compute. Lower effort reduced reported reasoning tokens, but did not speed up every issue.

No broader campaign was dispatched. See [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) for per-task failures, metrics, public contract support and independently verified evidence.
