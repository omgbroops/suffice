# Retain the opt-in prototype; broader evaluation remains necessary

The requested implementation and nine-attempt Sonion pilot are complete. V2 passed all three independently graded issues with $0.02084424 of Luna inference; original Suffice and native Codex each passed two. V2 was slower, used more calls/tokens, and never activated Sol advice, repair or takeover. The live evidence therefore supports a promising low-cost Luna execution path, not a demonstrated benefit from every newly implemented mechanism.

Keep the experimental entrypoint and features opt-in. The comparison deliberately measures complete systems and cannot separate model-price effects from harness improvements. Two tasks were historically exposed and the third was already prepared; this is not a fresh held-out result. No additional inference runs were started.
